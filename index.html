1990s pixel arcade · Space pinball for ages 6 and up

Midnight Circuit

Control the flippers, chain routes and challenge 60-second Fever and multiball.

Provided by Wen-Hsun Hsu / 17apphouse. There are no ads, purchases or developer-operated accounts. The complete offline game and local records do not require Google sign-in.

Start playing directly

Version 29 opens table selection, and ordinary Start plays the complete offline game. An active Google Play request leads directly to sign-in or offline choices. There is no country picker, self-reported age, or local eligibility/guardian-consent declaration. The app collects no birth date or exact age.

Optional Google Play leaderboard

Google handles PGS account sign-in, any required account/parent approval, and access to public leaderboard features. The app applies no custom regional or fixed-age cutoff. Successful sign-in is not proof of verified age, complete supervision checks, or compliance approval under every local law. Public ranks still depend on Google profile visibility and supervision restrictions. Eligible players worldwide use the same board for each table, without age- or region-separated boards.

Google Play Age Signals 0.0.4 is queried only for a player-requested optional Google feature or reconnection under saved opt-in. When sharing is requested, the player chooses in Google UI. Known Google requirements for verification or pending/declined significant-change approval cannot be ignored. Not-shared, unavailable or failed signals mean no usable signal, not verified age. Without a known block, PGS then handles account sign-in and service availability; there is no local-declaration shortcut. Raw age ranges, sources and approval information are processed briefly in memory, never saved, sent to leaderboards or used for analytics, and are not represented as age proof bound to the current PGS player. Identity binds only when sign-in is complete at game start; eligible completed scores are submitted automatically. Confirmation is not a public rank, and existing offline games are never imported.

Google services handle gamer identity, scores and SDK analytics/diagnostics. Having no developer-operated server does not mean Google processes no data. Read our privacy policy and deletion instructions.
