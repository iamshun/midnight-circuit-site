Start playing directly

Version 28 opens table selection and the ordinary Start button plays the complete offline game. Only an active request for Google Play asks for an applicable country or region and eligibility. The 249 choices are not inferred from language, IP or SIM. No birth date or exact age is stored.

Optional Google Play leaderboard

There is no fixed 16+ rule. The age to manage a Google Account is usually 13, with 14–16 exceptions in some locations; applicable consent rules also matter. Players locally declare that they meet the relevant threshold, understand the data use and have any required parent or legal-guardian consent. This is not Google age verification, complete supervision verification or worldwide legal approval. Below-threshold, unknown and undisclosed choices keep all offline play.

This version integrates Google Play Age Signals 0.0.4, requested only through an active choice and any required sharing consent. Raw age ranges and sharing/approval signals stay in memory for compliance eligibility; they are not saved, sent to leaderboards or used for analytics. Availability and sharing depend on Google, location and supervision, without a worldwide rollout guarantee. Unshared or unavailable signals may allow an explicit local-declaration fallback only without a known block; required verification, pending/declined approval and incompatible signals cannot be bypassed. Google separately controls PGS sign-in and public ranking, including profile visibility and supervised-profile restrictions. All eligible players share one board per table, without age-separated boards. Only explicit sign-in saves connection opt-in; identity binds at game start only after SDK sign-in completes, followed by automatic eligible-score submission. Confirmation is not a public rank. Settings can switch players; eligibility changes revoke opt-in and current-game binding, and withdrawing eligibility after SDK use requires closing and reopening.
