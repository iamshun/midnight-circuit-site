Control the flippers, chain routes and challenge 60-second Fever and multiball.

Provided by Wen-Hsun Hsu / 17apphouse. There are no ads, purchases or developer-operated accounts. The complete offline game and local records do not require Google sign-in.

Complete offline play

Every age choice includes all offline gameplay. From 1.0.2, each launch first asks for an age group with no answer selected. The choice stays on the device; no birth date is collected and this choice is not sent to Google.

Optional adult leaderboard

From 1.0.2, only players 18+ may separately choose Google Play Games sign-in. A cold start for an under-18 or undisclosed age does not initialize PGS. This is this version's product threshold, not a claim about a worldwide legal age.

Only eligible standard games started after sign-in and completed are submitted. Historical and offline-started games are not imported. Public ranking also depends on Play Games profile visibility.
