# Midnight Circuit website

Public product and support website for **午夜電路 Midnight Circuit**, developed by Wen-Hsun Hsu / 17apphouse.

- Plain HTML with Traditional Chinese, English and Japanese views.
- No game source code, build artifacts, player records or credentials are included.
- Support: 17apphouse@gmail.com.
- Version 29 (1.0.5) removes country, self-declared age and local eligibility declarations. Google manages PGS account access and public-profile availability; one global leaderboard remains per table.
- Age Signals 0.0.4 still respects mandatory verification and pending/declined approval. Unknown signals do not mean verified age, and Google sign-in is not worldwide legal approval. Complete offline gameplay stays available.
- The homepage does not claim that Google Play Games is available to every player or that review for children and families is complete.

**GitHub Pages is enabled from `main` and `/ (root)`, with HTTPS enforced.** On 24 October 2026, the homepage and privacy policy both returned HTTP 200 over HTTPS and matched the reviewed HTML files byte for byte. This confirms website delivery; it does not confirm OAuth publication, Google review, or universal Google Play Games access.

Each page supports `?lang=zh-TW`, `?lang=en` and `?lang=ja`.

Serve `index.html` directly. `.nojekyll` keeps GitHub Pages from applying Jekyll processing.

GitHub Pages hosting and Google OAuth domain/brand verification are separate steps. A live HTTPS website does not itself prove Google has verified the domain or branding.
