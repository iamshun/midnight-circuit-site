歷史版本28（1.0.4）使用明示國家／地區、本機資格與必要同意聲明，再查Age Signals；29移除這些本機選擇，不把舊答案當作Google資格。1.0.3（26）仍採當時固定16+與本機年齡組別；1.0.2（24）每次啟動先問年齡、採18+及另行登入；1.0.1及以前內測版沒有上述年齡隔離。新行為不追溯套用到舊包，29已提供內部測試，尚未正式上架。

Midnight Circuit Privacy Policy

Updated: 25 October 2026 · Operator: Wen-Hsun Hsu / 17apphouse

Privacy and support: 17apphouse@gmail.com. This policy covers the Android game and website. The game targets a mixed audience aged 6 and up. We do not claim certification by Google or compliance approval under every jurisdiction's child-privacy laws.

1. Versions, Google platform features and offline choice

Internal test version 29 (1.0.5) opens table selection; ordinary Start plays the complete offline game. An active Google Play Games (PGS) request leads directly to sign-in/offline information. There is no country picker, self-reported age, or local eligibility/guardian-consent declaration; language, IP and SIM do not choose a country. The app collects no birth date or exact age and does not treat a custom minimum age as Google eligibility.

This version retains Google Play Age Signals SDK 0.0.4, queried only for a requested optional Google feature or reconnection under valid saved opt-in. The player chooses in Google UI when sharing consent is required. Known requirements for verification or pending/declined significant-change approval still block new PGS operations; retry errors or non-sharing cannot erase them. SHARED is not filtered by a local region or fixed minimum age. Unknown, not-shared, unavailable or failed signals are not verified age, complete supervision checks or legal approval. Without a known block, PGS handles account sign-in and service availability, with no local-declaration shortcut. Raw age ranges, sources and approval signals are processed briefly in memory, never written to preferences, diagnostic captures or pending scores, sent to leaderboards, or used for analytics/advertising/tracking. This app does not treat installation-level signals as age proof bound to the current PGS player.

Google handles PGS account sign-in, any required account/parent approval, and public leaderboard availability. Successful sign-in does not guarantee a public rank or compliance with every local law. Google separately manages profile visibility and supervision restrictions; this game does not change them for you. Only explicit sign-in saves opt-in. Settings can switch players or withdraw optional connection, revoking current identity/game binding and requiring an explicit close and normal reopen when necessary. Already submitted or dispatched operations cannot be recalled, and an existing offline game is not converted into a platform game.

Historical version 28 (1.0.4) used an explicit region plus local eligibility/required-consent declaration before Age Signals. Version 29 removes these choices and does not treat old answers as Google eligibility. 1.0.3 (26) retains its former fixed 16+ rule and local age group; 1.0.2 (24) asks age on every launch with an 18+ threshold and separate sign-in; 1.0.1 and earlier internal builds had no such age separation. New behavior is not applied retroactively to old packages, and version 29 is available in internal testing, not production.

2. Local data

The device stores language, music/sound/volume, control/display preferences, PGS opt-in, the current platform-access policy marker, necessary Google restriction flags (not raw signals), local scores/session summaries, and diagnostic input/game/performance records. Version 29 no longer stores a country, age band, or local eligibility/consent declaration, and collects no birth date or exact age. Technical records are not automatically sent to the developer. Android backup and device services follow system settings; uninstalling is not guaranteed to prevent restoration of older-version data.

3. Optional PGS and Google data

After explicit sign-in or reconnection under valid saved opt-in, the game reads a PGS player ID. Signed-in players can open the native leaderboard. Only a new standard game whose SDK sign-in is complete at its start binds identity and submits an eligible completed score. Historical local records, abandoned games and games started offline are not imported; signing in later cannot bind an old game. Players worldwide share the same QBA board per table without age/region-separated boards. Pending/confirmed/error/offline results describe that session, not a public rank. Local pending scores may store identity, score, session/table context and retry state for the same account; they do not contain raw Google age signals.

Google discloses SDK processing of gamer identity (name/gamertag, player identification and avatar), analytics and diagnostics, plus game scores according to use. This differs from local technical records. The game has no ads, purchases, developer-operated account/game server, Email/Profile or friends-list requests, or cloud saves. No developer-operated server does not mean Google processes no data. You can avoid PGS by playing offline; this is not a separate switch for every automatic Google telemetry category after sign-in.

PGS uses HTTPS. See Google's SDK disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy. Public scores/ranks also depend on Play Games profile visibility and Google's rules. Task confirmation does not guarantee a public rank. Making a whole profile public can affect activity visibility in other games; this game does not change it for you.

4. Retention, deletion and contact

Local data remains until replaced, updated by bounded storage or cleared by you. We do not promise an unknown fixed retention period. Google manages its service data under its policies.

  1. To delete Google data for this game only, open your Play Games profile, go to Your Data and the deletion options, then choose Delete individual game data and find Midnight Circuit. Follow Google's current instructions.
  2. You need not delete your whole Google Account or Play Games profile. Google deletion does not clear local preferences, scores, pending submissions or support email. Android Settings → this app → Storage → clear app data removes local records/preferences and prevents an old pending score being retried.
  3. For access/deletion help regarding support correspondence we actually hold, email 17apphouse@gmail.com, for example with the subject “Midnight Circuit data deletion”. We handle requests according to identity checks, the data held and applicable obligations. We do not request passwords/codes or promise to delete all Google data or finish within an unverified fixed period.

5. Website and support services

This site uses GitHub Pages and adds no analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, external fonts or forms. GitHub may still process IP addresses, browser/connection information and request logs to provide, protect and operate its service under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Mail providers process support messages you choose to send for replies/troubleshooting, not advertising campaigns. Avoid unnecessary personal data.

We update this policy/date when features, providers or purposes change. These disclosures are not guarantees of error-free service, zero collection or Google review approval.
