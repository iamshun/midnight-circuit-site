1. Versions, regional eligibility and offline choice

Internal test version 28 (1.0.4) opens table selection; ordinary Start plays the complete offline game. Only the first active Google Play Games (PGS) request asks you to select an applicable country or region from 249 explicit choices, then neutrally declare eligibility or choose unknown. Language, IP, SIM and device settings do not select a region for you. No birth date or exact age is stored, and we do not ask Google for a birth date. The age to manage a Google Account is usually 13, with 14–16 exceptions in some locations; local consent requirements also apply. This is not a fixed worldwide 16+ rule.

Players locally declare that they meet the relevant threshold, understand the data use and have any applicable parent or legal-guardian consent. This is not Google age verification, complete supervision verification or approval under every local law. Below-threshold, unknown and undisclosed local choices keep all offline content without starting PGS. This version integrates Google Play Age Signals SDK 0.0.4, queried only after an explicit player request and any required Google sharing consent. Google may return not-shared, verification-required, an error or no usable data; availability in every country or device is not assumed. Raw age ranges, sources and approval signals are used only in memory for compliance eligibility: never stored, put in troubleshooting captures or pending queues, sent to leaderboards, or used for analytics, ads or tracking. Unshared or unavailable signals are not Google eligibility approval. Explicit local declaration is an option only without a known block; required verification, pending/declined approval or incompatible signals cannot be bypassed. A local declaration is not a Google signal or age verification. Google separately controls PGS sign-in and public ranking, including supervised-profile restrictions and visibility. Local eligibility does not guarantee Google sign-in or a public rank. All eligible players use the same board for the same table.

Only explicit sign-in saves connection opt-in; eligible opted-in players may get reconnection attempts on later cold launches. Settings can switch players or choose region and eligibility again, revoking opt-in, current identity and the current-game ranking binding. Withdrawing eligibility after an SDK request blocks new developer-issued platform operations, saves the choice and requires an explicit close before normal reopening. This cannot recall data already submitted or dispatched, or turn an offline-started game into a ranked game.

An old 16+ answer is not inferred to be a new regional eligibility or consent declaration; the new confirmation is required. Earlier 1.0.3 (26) retains its fixed 16+ and local age-group flow. 1.0.2 (24) asked for age at each launch and used an 18+ threshold with separate sign-in. Internal versions 1.0.1 and below had no such age separation. The new behavior does not apply retroactively.

2. Local data

The device stores language, music/sound/volume, control and display preferences, selected region, eligibility and required-consent choices, necessary eligibility-block flags (not raw signals), PGS connection opt-in, local scores and game summaries, plus input, gameplay and performance records for troubleshooting. No birth date or exact age is stored. Local technical records are not automatically sent to the developer. Android backup and device-level services follow system settings; uninstalling does not guarantee that system backups cannot later restore data.

3. Optional PGS and Google data

After an eligible player explicitly signs in, or reconnects using saved opt-in, the game reads the PGS player ID. A new standard game binds identity only when connection and SDK sign-in are complete at its start, then automatically submits an eligible completed score; Google's native leaderboard is also available. Historical local scores, abandoned games and offline-started games are not imported, and later sign-in does not bind an earlier game. The end screen shows that game's pending, confirmed, error or offline status; confirmation is not a public rank. Account-bound pending records may store the player ID, score, game/table context and retry state locally. The selected region and local eligibility/consent choices are not included in the Google score-submission API.

Google discloses SDK processing of gamer identity (name/gamertag, player identification and avatar), analytics and diagnostics, plus game scores according to use. This differs from local technical records. The game has no ads, purchases, developer-operated account/game server, Email/Profile or friends-list requests, or cloud saves. No developer-operated server does not mean Google processes no data. You can avoid PGS by playing offline; this is not a separate switch for every automatic Google telemetry category after sign-in.

PGS uses HTTPS. See Google's SDK disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy. Public scores/ranks also depend on Play Games profile visibility and Google's rules. Task confirmation does not guarantee a public rank. Making a whole profile public can affect activity visibility in other games; this game does not change it for you.

4. Retention, deletion and contact

Local data remains until replaced, updated by bounded storage or cleared by you. We do not promise an unknown fixed retention period. Google manages its service data under its policies.

  1. To delete Google data for this game only, open your Play Games profile, go to Your Data and the deletion options, then choose Delete individual game data and find Midnight Circuit. Follow Google's current instructions.
  2. You need not delete your whole Google Account or Play Games profile. Google deletion does not clear local preferences, scores, pending submissions or support email. Android Settings → this app → Storage → clear app data removes local records/preferences and prevents an old pending score being retried.
  3. For access/deletion help regarding support correspondence we actually hold, email 17apphouse@gmail.com, for example with the subject “Midnight Circuit data deletion”. We handle requests according to identity checks, the data held and applicable obligations. We do not request passwords/codes or promise to delete all Google data or finish within an unverified fixed period.

GitHub may still process IP addresses, browser/connection information and request logs to provide, protect and operate its service under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Mail providers process support messages you choose to send for replies/troubleshooting, not advertising campaigns. Avoid unnecessary personal data.
