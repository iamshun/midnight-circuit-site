Midnight Circuit 隱私政策

更新日期：2026-10-24 · 提供者：Wen-Hsun Hsu / 17apphouse

隱私與支援：17apphouse@gmail.com。本政策說明Android遊戲及網站資料處理。遊戲面向6歲以上的混合受眾；不宣稱已取得Google、兒童法規或所有地區的合規認證。

1. 版本、年齡與離線選擇

1.0.2（24）起，每次開啟先以沒有預選答案的中性畫面詢問「未滿18／18以上／不提供」。只把年齡組別存於本機，不收生日、不向Google查詢年齡、不傳送此選擇。未滿18與未知年齡在冷啟動時不初始化PGS，仍可遊玩完整離線內容及保存本機成績。18+只是本版線上功能的保守產品門檻，不是全球法律年齡聲明。

成人須再明確選擇登入，才啟動Google Play Games。設定中可重新選年齡；若本次已嘗試線上登入後降為低齡／未知，遊戲先封鎖新的自有平台作業、儲存選擇並要求明示關閉，再正常開啟。這不撤回已提交資料，也不把正在玩的離線局轉為平台局。

較早的1.0.1及以前內測版只有可選PGS登入，沒有上述年齡隔離；不能把新措施描述為所有舊版已有。兒童／未知年齡請使用1.0.2起的分流版本；未取得該版前，不使用舊版的線上登入。

2. 本機資料

裝置保存語言、音樂／音效／音量、操作與顯示偏好、年齡組別、本機高分與局次摘要，以及供排錯的輸入、遊戲事件與效能紀錄。這些本機技術紀錄不會自動寄給開發者。Android備份及裝置層服務另依系統設定；不保證卸載後絕不由系統備份恢復。

3. 可選PGS與Google資料

合格成人主動登入後，遊戲讀取PGS玩家ID，提交登入後開始且完成的合格標準局分數，並可開啟Google排行榜。舊本機成績、放棄局及離線開始的局不補送。同帳號待送紀錄可能在本機保存玩家ID、分數、局次／桌別及重試狀態；年齡組別不附在Google送分API中。

Google官方披露SDK會處理遊戲身份（名稱／gamertag、玩家識別與頭像）、分析及診斷資料，並依功能處理遊戲分數。這與本機技術紀錄不同。我們沒有廣告、購買、自建帳號／遊戲伺服器、Email/Profile或好友清單要求、雲端存檔功能；沒有自架伺服器不等於Google沒有處理資料。選擇不登入可使用完整離線遊戲；不代表登入後可逐項關閉Google自動遙測。

PGS使用HTTPS傳輸，參閱 Google SDK資料披露與 Google隱私政策。公開分數／名次也依Play Games profile可見性及Google規則；Task確認不保證公開名次。公開整個profile可能影響其他遊戲的活動顯示，遊戲不會替你更改此設定。

4. 保存、刪除與聯絡

本機資料保留至後續紀錄取代、有界儲存更新或由你清除。沒有未知的固定保存天數承諾；Google資料依其服務政策管理。

  1. 刪除Google端本遊戲資料：開啟 Play Games profile，進入「你的資料」與資料刪除項目，再選「刪除個別遊戲資料」，找到Midnight Circuit。以 Google當時的官方步驟為準。
  2. 不必刪整個Google帳號或整個Play Games profile。 Google端刪除不會同時清掉本機偏好、成績、待送分或支援郵件。清除本機資料可在Android設定→本App→儲存空間→清除App資料；這會移除本機紀錄與偏好，也可避免舊pending日後再送。
  3. 如需協助存取／刪除我們實際持有的支援往來，請寄 17apphouse@gmail.com，可用「Midnight Circuit 資料刪除」為主旨。我們依可核對身份、實際持有資料及適用義務處理，不要求密碼／驗證碼，不承諾代刪Google所有資料或固定完成天數。

5. 網站與支援服務

網站使用GitHub Pages；本頁沒有自有分析、追蹤像素、cookies、外部字型或表單。GitHub仍可能為提供、保護及營運服務而處理IP、瀏覽器／連線與請求紀錄，依 GitHub隱私聲明處理。主動寄出的支援郵件由郵件服務處理，用於回覆與排錯，不用於廣告行銷。請勿寄送不必要個資。

功能、供應商或用途改變時會更新本政策與日期。這些資料處理說明不構成絕對無錯誤、零資料蒐集或Google審查通過的保證。

Midnight Circuit Privacy Policy

Updated: 24 October 2026 · Operator: Wen-Hsun Hsu / 17apphouse

Privacy and support: 17apphouse@gmail.com. This policy covers the Android game and website. The game targets a mixed audience aged 6 and up. We do not claim certification by Google or compliance approval under every jurisdiction's child-privacy laws.

1. Versions, age and offline choice

Starting with 1.0.2 (24), each launch asks for Under 18 / 18 or older / Prefer not to say on a neutral screen with no answer selected. Only an age group is stored locally. We do not collect a birth date, query Google for age, or send this choice to Google. A cold start for under-18 or unknown age does not initialize PGS; all offline gameplay and local records remain available. The 18+ online threshold is this version's conservative product policy, not a worldwide legal-age claim.

Adults must separately choose sign-in before Google Play Games starts. The choice can be changed in Settings. If an online sign-in was already requested and the choice is lowered, the game blocks new developer-issued platform operations, saves the choice and requires an explicit close before normal reopening. This cannot recall data already submitted or turn an ongoing offline game into a ranked game.

Earlier internal versions, 1.0.1 and below, offered optional PGS without this age separation. The new protection must not be assumed to exist in old versions. Children or users not providing age should use the 1.0.2-or-later separation; until available, do not use old-version online sign-in.

2. Local data

The device stores language, music/sound/volume, control and display preferences, age group, local scores and game summaries, plus input, gameplay and performance records for troubleshooting. Local technical records are not automatically sent to the developer. Android backup and device-level services follow system settings; uninstalling does not guarantee that system backups cannot later restore data.

3. Optional PGS and Google data

After an eligible adult chooses sign-in, the game reads the PGS player ID, submits eligible standard games started after sign-in and completed, and can open Google's leaderboard. Historical local scores, abandoned games and offline-started games are not imported. Account-bound pending records may store the player ID, score, game/table context and retry state locally. The age group is not included in the Google score-submission API.

Google discloses SDK processing of gamer identity (name/gamertag, player identification and avatar), analytics and diagnostics, plus game scores according to use. This differs from local technical records. The game has no ads, purchases, developer-operated account/game server, Email/Profile or friends-list requests, or cloud saves. No developer-operated server does not mean Google processes no data. You can avoid PGS by playing offline; this is not a separate switch for every automatic Google telemetry category after sign-in.

PGS uses HTTPS. See Google's SDK disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy. Public scores/ranks also depend on Play Games profile visibility and Google's rules. Task confirmation does not guarantee a public rank. Making a whole profile public can affect activity visibility in other games; this game does not change it for you.

4. Retention, deletion and contact

Local data remains until replaced, updated by bounded storage or cleared by you. We do not promise an unknown fixed retention period. Google manages its service data under its policies.

  1. To delete Google data for this game only, open your Play Games profile, go to Your Data and the deletion options, then choose Delete individual game data and find Midnight Circuit. Follow Google's current instructions.
  2. You need not delete your whole Google Account or Play Games profile. Google deletion does not clear local preferences, scores, pending submissions or support email. Android Settings → this app → Storage → clear app data removes local records/preferences and prevents an old pending score being retried.
  3. For access/deletion help regarding support correspondence we actually hold, email 17apphouse@gmail.com, for example with the subject “Midnight Circuit data deletion”. We handle requests according to identity checks, the data held and applicable obligations. We do not request passwords/codes or promise to delete all Google data or finish within an unverified fixed period.

5. Website and support services

This site uses GitHub Pages and adds no analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, external fonts or forms. GitHub may still process IP addresses, browser/connection information and request logs to provide, protect and operate its service under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Mail providers process support messages you choose to send for replies/troubleshooting, not advertising campaigns. Avoid unnecessary personal data.

We update this policy/date when features, providers or purposes change. These disclosures are not guarantees of error-free service, zero collection or Google review approval.
